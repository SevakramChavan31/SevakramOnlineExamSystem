Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to some of the most common questions.

Use the Student Login option and enter the email address and password associated with your student account.

After logging in, open your Student Dashboard and select an available examination. Read the examination instructions and start the exam.

The examination can be automatically submitted when the configured examination duration expires.

After completing an examination, the available result information can be viewed from the student dashboard or result section.

Use the "Forgot Password?" option on the login page and follow the password reset process.

Administrators can log in through Admin Login and manage examinations, questions, students and reports from the admin portal.
