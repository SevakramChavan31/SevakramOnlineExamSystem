Platform Features

Designed for students and administrators, our examination system makes every step of the assessment process easier.

Secure Login

Secure authentication for students and administrators with role-based access to the examination portal.

Online Exams

Students can view available examinations, read instructions and attend exams directly through the online portal.

Time Management

Examination duration and countdown functionality help students complete their exams within the allocated time.

Automatic Evaluation

Objective questions can be evaluated automatically to calculate marks, correct answers and wrong answers.

Performance Reports

Students and administrators can view scores, percentages, pass/fail status and examination performance.

Admin Reports

Administrators can analyze examination attempts, student performance and export report data.

Question Management

Administrators can manage questions, options, marks and correct answers for different examinations.

Admin Dashboard

Administrators get a centralized dashboard to manage exams, students, questions and reports.

Responsive Design

The platform is designed to work smoothly across desktops, tablets and mobile devices.
